---
title: Roasted red potatoes
---

## Ingredients

Serves _two_.

- eight red potatoes per person
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- 1/2 tsp salt
- 1/4 tsp pepper
- 1/2 tsp dried rosemary (optional but recommended)

## Instructions

1. Preheat oven to 450F
1. Cut potatoes in half or quarters, as long as all the pieces are about the same size
1. In a large mixing bowl, toss potatoes with olive oil, salt, pepper, and optional rosemary
1. Empty potatoes onto a large roasting pan (a pan with shallow sides. a cookie sheet will work too)
1. If you want lots of browning, orient the potatoes so that a cut edge (not the skin) is touching the roasting pan surface
1. Bake the potatoes in the oven for about 20-30 minutes.
1. Serve
